# On-page SEO — eight local pages

Evidence: `../technical-evidence.json`. Scope is the current generated local build; no keyword volume, live SERP, click-through or ranking measurements were made.

## Measured baseline

- 8/8 pages have unique, nonempty French titles, 41–54 characters.
- 8/8 have unique, nonempty meta descriptions, 128–171 characters.
- 8/8 have exactly one H1 and `lang="fr"`.
- 8/8 include canonical, robots, Open Graph, Twitter preview and viewport metadata in initial HTML.
- All canonical page links are discoverable from the homepage in at most one click in this build.
- Important case context, objectives, delivered work and video summaries are server-rendered.
- No broken local fragment destinations or discovered local resource URLs were found.

These are passed implementation checks, not proof of ranking, snippet selection or content strength.

## Low-priority snippet improvement

The Prompolu web case description is 171 characters; shortening its lead clause may improve the chance of showing the useful client/scope information before truncation. Character length is a presentation heuristic, not a hard Google limit or an SEO error.

## Content/intent opportunity

The home, services and portfolio H1s are editorial slogans (“Votre marque mérite d’être remarquée”, “Du sens dans les idées…”, “Des idées qui prennent forme”). Supporting text and metadata do identify the agency, location and services, so there is no missing-keyword blocker. Consider making one visible supporting heading more explicit about the service/audience on each landing page and strengthening individual case descriptions with user-confirmed decisions, challenges and outcomes. Avoid stuffing the main headlines or inventing results.

Keep existing direct anchors to permanent case pages; they are materially stronger for discovery than modal-only buttons.

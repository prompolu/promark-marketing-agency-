# Search experience / intent review — local qualitative assessment

## Scope

The local homepage title suggests a commercial/local-agency theme: “Agence marketing à Fès : web & motion design | Promark” (`scripts/seo.mjs:10`). This review did not fetch a live SERP or keyword-volume dataset. Therefore it cannot establish a dominant result type, rankability, keyword demand, competitor depth or a real page-type mismatch. No numerical SXO Gap Score or SERP-derived persona scores are assigned. The user stories below are task-based hypotheses drawn from the supplied business brief and visible page content, not invented search observations.

## Task-based review

| Visitor task | Local support | Remaining friction |
|---|---|---|
| Identify what the agency does and where it operates | Homepage opening paragraph says marketing/creation in Fès; titles and About repeat the location naturally. | Editorial H1 is evocative; the nearby concrete sentence is important and should remain visible. |
| Decide whether a service fits an actual need | Services lists four offerings, outputs, scope agreements and sur-devis expectations. | All services share one page and the descriptions are brief; deeper service-specific questions are not answered yet. Separate pages are an opportunity only if useful content and actual keyword/intent research justify them. |
| Judge the quality and relevance of work | Three permanent case pages, two real clients and six supplied videos; Prompolu case links to its website. | Cases explain outputs but not the brief, creative/technical choices, challenges or results. |
| Know who will handle a project | About explains the agency approach and collaboration stages. | No approved practitioner profile or concrete project-management example. Do not invent a team or credentials. |
| Make an enquiry on mobile | Contact provides email/call/WhatsApp links; form explains mail-app draft behavior and download fallback. | This is not an in-page delivery service. A user without a configured email app must use the direct email/WhatsApp or fallback; a hosted delivery integration could reduce that friction if authorised later. |

## Recommendations

1. Add client-specific decisions and delivery evidence to the case studies before expanding to generic “SEO tips”.
2. Make service detail more decision-useful: fit, required inputs, typical deliverables and boundaries, supported by relevant real work.
3. Keep local/categorical clarity next to editorial headings and use meaningful case subtitles, without keyword stuffing.
4. Add actual agency/practitioner detail approved by the owner.
5. Once deployed, research the real target queries and local results before deciding on new service/location pages or assigning an SXO score.

Privacy/trust opportunity: the site asks for enquiry information that a visitor sends via their own email app. No privacy/legal page appears in the eight-page navigation. Have the owner approve an accurate explanation of how enquiries are handled if appropriate; do not fabricate legal identity, retention terms or compliance claims. This is a trust/compliance consideration, not a demonstrated search-ranking penalty.

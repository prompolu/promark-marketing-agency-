# Content, local identity and search experience

Marketing/creative agency based in Fès, Morocco. Real named clients and supplied films support the portfolio; address, founders, reviews, certifications and numeric outcomes were not supplied.

Fixed: four plain-language service explanations; two service-specific questions each; proof descriptions that state visible deliverables rather than internal verification caveats; enquiry-information explanation linked near every form. Each service retains audience, preparation, deliverables, scope and contact options. The requested single portfolio CTA remains intact.

A user can compare service scope, inspect a relevant case, understand what to prepare and start an enquiry. Content is server-rendered/static with one H1 per route. No bulk keyword/city pages, filler target word counts, invented authors or commercial FAQ rich-result claims were added.

Remaining authority work depends on actual evidence: approved client quotes, measured case outcomes with dates/methodology, real team details and verified business/social profiles. GBP/reviews were not measured. The exact street address is unknown; Organization + city/country is used rather than an invented LocalBusiness address. No ranking/lead improvement is claimed.

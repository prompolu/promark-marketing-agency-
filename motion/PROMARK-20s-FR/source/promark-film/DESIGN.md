# PROMARK — editorial agency film
## Source
Read-only copy of origin/promark-preview: public/style.css, public/logo.svg, public/media, and preview/PROMARK-preview.zip/index.html. Original working tree is untouched. Stylesheet itself identifies its palette as provisional; these are its actual current tokens, not a claim of final brand approval.
## Style Prompt
Bold editorial agency film. Warm paper, ink, electric lime. Oversized type, thin rules, generous alignment, an eight-spoke asterisk and a curved underline. Black logo panels use the unmodified supplied negative-space SVG, with paper beneath its transparent lettering. Portfolio photography retains its own colors. Short energetic travel, readable holds, then complete stillness.
## Colors
- Paper #f5f4ee — primary canvas and reverse type
- Ink #141515 — primary foreground and dark surfaces
- Lime #d7ff00 — service emphasis and transition surfaces
- Muted #62645c — supporting copy on paper
- Line #d7d8cf — hairline rules on paper
- Black #000000 — original logo canvas, required for seamless SVG placement
## Typography
Website family stack Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif; source italic highlight Georgia, serif. Local editable render embeds Liberation Sans (Arial metric-compatible substitute) and Noto Serif italic (Georgia substitute); licensed original font binaries were not supplied. Film uses 120px opening type, 110px service headings, 68px numbered rows, 42px process labels, 46px CTA.
## Motion
One paused deterministic GSAP master. 20 seconds / 30fps / 600 frames. Dot-to-circle inversion, asterisk-driven push, strip-to-row shutter, arrow-to-gallery, accelerated card rail, perspective project selection, card-to-rule, node-to-button, button-to-final surface. Last frame fully settled at 17.8s.
## What NOT to Do
- Never alter SVG paths or split logo letters.
- Never invent clients, results, dashboards, contacts, or addresses.
- Never apply Prompolu bronze to the agency identity.
- No continuous spinning; no fake form success; no platform outro.
- No blur on readable text holds; no fade to black.
